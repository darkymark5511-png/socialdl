# DESIGN.md — SocialDL

## Identity

- Product / project name: SocialDL
- One-line what it is: Extract and download public media from major social platforms
- Audience: people who need a fast, no-account grab of public clips and images
- Personality keywords: sharp, utilitarian, late-night tool, precise, dry

## Visual language

- Reference feel: Linear app chrome, Raycast dense panels, Arc browser accent restraint
- Overall mood: bold but controlled (dark-first utility)

## Palette

- Core colors:
  - Primary: #E8FF47 (acid lime — action only)
  - Secondary: #7C5CFF (violet — secondary emphasis)
  - Neutral base: #0B0C0F background, #14161A surface, #1C1F26 elevated, #2A2E38 border
- Accent: #E8FF47 on primary buttons, focus rings, active platform chips
- Forbidden / avoid: soft pastels, purple-to-pink gradients, glassmorphism stacks, generic blue CTA

## Typography

- Heading font: Space Grotesk — geometric, tight tracking on large titles
- Body font: Inter — readable at small sizes for forms and logs
- Monospace: JetBrains Mono for hashes, timings, status codes in admin

## Layout & rhythm

- Preferred density: tight (tool density, not marketing air)
- Section rhythm: consistent with breaks (hero strip, then work panel, then result)
- Constraints: max-width 720px for the downloader work surface; full bleed dark field behind

## Motion

- Motion level: subtle
- Allowed: 150–200ms hover on chips/buttons, soft opacity on result reveal
- Forbidden: parallax, continuous gradient animation, bounce

## Dials

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Content constraints

- Real claims / stats: none — no fabricated numbers
- Voice: plain, short, no hype adjectives ("lightning", "blazing", "seamless")
- Never appear: marketing hero claims, stock photos, fake social proof

## Notes

- Platform filter is a first-class control, not a footer badge row
- Admin stays functional dense table, same palette
- Prefer hard edges and 1px borders over soft shadows
